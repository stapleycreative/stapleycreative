Signal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

What Signal does

Signal is a Chrome extension that analyzes the visual design properties of webpages and presents a personality-type reading of what the page is projecting.

What data Signal reads

When you click "Read this page," Signal reads computed CSS styles, layout metrics, contrast ratios, and visual properties from the current tab. This includes text content, font sizes, colors, and spacing values.

What Signal stores

Signal stores a local reading diary using Chrome's storage API. All data stays on your device and never leaves your browser.

What Signal does NOT do

Signal does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. No data is sent to external servers. No analytics, tracking, or telemetry. No cookies. No remote code.

Data deletion

Clear your reading diary anytime from within the extension. Uninstalling removes all stored data.
